🛒 Anchor example for this unit: the same Pune online shopping app
  • Neural network: learns to spot fraudulent payments and to forecast demand.
  • Bayesian methods: update the probability that a customer defaults as new payments arrive.
  • Time series: forecasts next month's sales, including the festival-season spike.
  • Rule induction and decision trees: give readable churn rules such as "complaints > 2 and no purchase in 60 days".
  • PCA: squeezes hundreds of correlated customer features into a few.
  • KNN: recommends what similar customers bought.
  • Stochastic search: tunes model settings by random sampling instead of trying every combination.
  • Correlation and regression: link ad spend, discounts and footfall to sales.

1. Using the tracker

  • Tap a question to open or close its answer. Questions tagged no answer yet have nothing to open.
  • ✓ marks a question Completed. ↻ marks it Needs revision. The counters and progress bar update at once.
  • Tags under each question show its level (Basic, Moderate, Hard) and Bloom level. "(inferred)" means the bank didn't print the level, so it was guessed from the verb. Numerical questions carry a Numerical tag and show their dataset under the question.
  • Q24 and Q26 are the same question. They carry a same answer as Q# tag, so adding the answer to either one shows it on both.
  • Use the chips All / Pending / Completed / Revise / Numerical to filter, and the search box to find a question by words or number (e.g. 31).
  • Reset progress (under the filters) clears only your ✓/↻ marks. Your answers stay.
Everything is saved only in this browser on this device. Opening the page on another device or from another link starts empty. Use Backup / restore to move your marks and answers across.

2. Adding answers

  1. On the Tracker tab, click ＋ Add answers (JSON).
  2. Paste the JSON into the box, or click 📄 Choose .json file(s) and pick one or more files.
  3. Click Add to page for pasted JSON. Files are added as soon as you pick them.

If a question already has an answer, the new one replaces it. If any item is invalid, nothing is imported and an error tells you why.

  "answers": [

  ]

These two shorter shapes also work:

[ { "q": 1, "answer": "..." } ]

{ "q": 1, "answer": "..." }

Rules

  • q (or qno / question_no) is the question number, 1 to 34.
  • answer is a non-empty string of Markdown. Write line breaks as \n inside the string.
  • Extra fields such as unit or topic are ignored.
  • A wrapping ```json fence around the JSON is stripped automatically.
